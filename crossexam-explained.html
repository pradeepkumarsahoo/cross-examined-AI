Now imagine a distributor receiving thousands of shipments a month, each with 5 to 7 papers in different formats: scanned PDFs, phone photos, Excel sheets and government JSON files. Nobody can compare them all carefully, so mistakes get paid. CrossExam does that comparison automatically.

The business problem

Before paying a supplier, the Accounts Payable (AP) team must confirm three things. Finance teams call this 3-way matching: Purchase Order ↔ Goods Receipt ↔ Invoice, plus the supporting documents.

📝

We ordered it

The invoice's items, quantities and prices match the Purchase Order (PO).

📦

We received it

The billed quantity matches what the warehouse counted in the Goods Receipt Note (GRN).

⚖️

The papers agree and comply

The packing list, lorry receipt, e-way bill and quality certificates tell the same story, and the tax rate and shelf life are correct.

What goes wrong when it's done by hand

Money lost

Overpayment

Paying for cartons that never arrived, a price quietly raised above the PO, or the wrong GST rate.

Money lost

Double payment

The same invoice comes in as a PDF and later as a scanned copy, and both get paid.

Time lost

Slow month-end close

Clean invoices wait in the same queue as problem ones. Suppliers chase payments and staff work overtime.

Risk

Fraud & compliance

Tampered invoices, e-way bill mismatches (a tax red flag) and short-expiry stock slip through.

The hidden inefficiency: most shipments are perfectly clean, so people spend most of their effort confirming that nothing is wrong. The real goal is to approve the clean ones automatically and send people only the exceptions, already investigated.

How agentic AI helps

Regular automation follows fixed rules and breaks on messy input. A plain chatbot can read but can't act or check its work. Agentic AI means AI agents that pursue a goal: they decide which tools to use, gather evidence, and hand back a structured result. They do this inside limits that code sets.

🧾 Manual process

  • A person opens every paper
  • Retypes numbers into the ERP
  • Checks the totals by eye
  • Emails the supplier from scratch
  • Works the same way on clean and problem shipments

⚙️ Traditional OCR / RPA

  • Template per supplier layout; breaks when a layout changes
  • Struggles with phone photos and multi-document scans
  • Can flag a mismatch, but can't explain why
  • No investigation and no proposed fix

🤖 Agentic AI (CrossExam)

  • Reads any layout and format, including scans and photos
  • Splits a mixed scan into separate documents
  • Investigates problems using tools: PO, GRN, supplier history, similar past cases
  • Proposes the fix and drafts the supplier email
  • Waits for a person before anything is paid or sent

The one design rule

🤖 AI reads

It understands messy documents and investigates problems. This is where people are slow and fixed rules fail.

🧮 Code counts

Totals, tolerances, matching and every rupee are plain code that agents call as a tool. The results are exact, free and auditable.

👤 People decide

Anything that pays money or contacts a supplier waits for human approval, with role limits on the amount.

The agent team

There is no single giant prompt. Several small specialists each do one job, just as an AP department has different roles. Cheap models do the easy work, and the expensive model is used only where judgement is needed.

Small model

Classifier

"What is this file?" Recognises invoices, packing lists, lorry receipts and so on, and splits a 3-in-1 scan into three documents.

Small → strong

Extractors

One per document type. They fill a strict form (invoice no., GSTIN, lines, totals). If the code checks fail, the document is re-read by a stronger model that is told what failed.

Small model

Supervisor

Looks at the match result and decides whether to auto-approve, investigate, or send the pack to a person. It can call the Reconciler.

Mid model

Reconciler

Finds stray papers that belong to the shipment (e.g. a certificate filed separately) and re-runs the match.

Strong model

Exception resolver

The investigator. It checks the PO and GRN, the supplier's history and similar past cases, then proposes a fix and drafts the supplier email.

Not an agent

ERP export

Deliberately not an AI tool. Plain code exports each approved invoice exactly once.

Why "agentic" matters for the investigation step: a shortage can have many explanations. Maybe a second lorry is still on the way. Maybe this supplier is short every month. Maybe a credit note has already been issued. The exception agent chooses which tools to call to find out, the way an experienced AP executive would, and writes up what it found.

Guardrails: why finance can trust it

AI that touches money needs limits. Every agent runs inside the same safety layers.

✋ Human approval for actions

The agent can propose a payment or email but cannot execute it. Amounts above ₹5 lakh need a finance manager, not an AP executive.

🛡️ Prompt-injection guard

Instructions hidden in a document are removed before any model sees them, and the pack is flagged for a person.

🔒 Code has the last word

An agent can make a decision stricter (send it to a person) but never looser (it can't approve something the engine blocked).

💸 Never pay twice

Four layers protect against it: file fingerprints, invoice dedupe, safe task claims after crashes, and an export that refuses repeats.

🙈 Data masking

Bank account and IFSC numbers are masked before reaching any model.

📊 Cost & confidence

Every AI call is logged with its cost. If the model isn't confident in what it read, a person checks it.

Who benefits

The same system serves different people in different ways.

AP executive

Stops retyping and cross-checking clean invoices. Their queue holds only real problems, each with evidence and a draft answer.

Finance manager / CFO

Less leakage from short shipments, price creep and duplicates. Faster month-end close, and an audit trail of who approved what.

Procurement & quality

Supplier patterns become visible: who ships short, who raises prices, whose certificates are missing.

Suppliers

Clean invoices are paid faster. Disputes arrive as a clear, evidence-backed email instead of a vague hold.
